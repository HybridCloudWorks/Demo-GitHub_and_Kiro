# L0 · Getting Started

> **Level:** L0 · **Estimated time:** 5 min · **Prerequisites:** none

Welcome. This is the very beginning of the course, and it assumes you know nothing about Git,
GitHub, or Kiro yet. That's the right place to start, and there's no shortcut worth skipping this
part for.

By the end of Level 0 you'll have a GitHub account, the tools installed on your own computer, and
your first saved change recorded in version control. That last one is a bigger deal than it
sounds. Once you've made a single commit, the mental model for everything that follows clicks into
place a lot faster.

## What you'll walk away with

- A working understanding of what version control is, and why anyone bothered to invent it
- A free GitHub account that becomes your home base for the rest of the course
- Git and Kiro installed and confirmed working on your machine
- Your first repository, with a real commit in its history that you made yourself

## The lessons in this level

1. [What is version control?](./what-is-version-control.md) - the core idea, in plain language.
2. [Create your GitHub account](./create-github-account.md) - where your work will live online.
3. [Install Git and Kiro](./install-git-and-kiro.md) - the two tools you'll lean on throughout.
4. [Your first repository and commit](./first-repo-and-commit.md) - the moment it becomes real.

:::tip Take your time
Nothing here is a race. Each lesson is short and ends with a quick checkpoint so you can be sure
the thing you just set up actually works before you move on. Setup problems are much cheaper to
fix now than three levels from now.
:::
